                Sun at this bed

                How much sun does it get?

                Stand where the bed is. Aim the cross at the top of trees, roofs and fences, sweeping slowly from east, through south, to west. Where the sky is open, aim at the horizon. Nothing is recorded or uploaded; the camera is only a viewfinder.
